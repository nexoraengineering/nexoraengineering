5V Regulated Power Supply PCB

An electronics hardware project focused on designing a regulated 5V power supply and implementing the circuit on a printed circuit board for reliable DC power delivery.

01 — OBJECTIVE

Project Objective

To design and implement a regulated power supply capable of providing a stable 5V DC output for electronic circuits.

02 — TECHNOLOGY

Tools & Technology

Electronic circuit design, PCB development and circuit analysis techniques were used for the power supply system.

03 — METHODOLOGY

Project Workflow

Transformer stage, rectification, filtering, voltage regulation, circuit analysis and PCB implementation were carried out during the project.

04 — RESULT

Key Result

A regulated 5V DC power supply design was developed with the required circuit stages and PCB implementation.
