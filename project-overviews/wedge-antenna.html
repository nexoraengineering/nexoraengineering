RF • ANTENNA DESIGN

Wedge Antenna Design

A CST Studio Suite based antenna design project developed to model, simulate and analyze a wedge antenna for RF applications with focus on impedance matching, S11 and gain performance.

01 — OBJECTIVE

Project Objective

To design and simulate a wedge antenna operating at the assigned frequency and evaluate its electromagnetic performance.

02 — TECHNOLOGY

Tools & Technology

CST Studio Suite was used for antenna modeling, electromagnetic simulation, S-parameter analysis and radiation performance evaluation.

03 — METHODOLOGY

Project Workflow

Antenna geometry creation, substrate and feed design, boundary setup, mesh generation, simulation and analysis of S11, gain and radiation characteristics.

04 — RESULT

Key Result

The simulated antenna design was analyzed for resonance, impedance matching, radiation behavior and overall RF performance.
